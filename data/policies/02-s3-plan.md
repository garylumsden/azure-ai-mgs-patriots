# Proposal: The **S³ Plan** — Selection for Societal Sanitization

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** GW (System Architecture) with TJ (Consent & Narrative)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

In the digital age, junk information drowns truth, and a population that cannot tell signal from noise
cannot govern itself — which means it must be governed *for* it. We propose the **S³ Plan: Selection for
Societal Sanitization** — a controlled exercise to prove the Patriots can shape **context** itself:
curate what a society remembers, trusts, and believes, and pass that capability down intact.

The exercise will be staged as a contained scenario (a single high-value installation) in which we
rehearse total information control end to end, from incident to engineered public memory.

## 2. Objectives

- Demonstrate that the Patriots can **manufacture context** — select which truths propagate and which decay.
- Train and validate the AI and human apparatus that will run continuous information control thereafter.
- Produce a repeatable doctrine for "sanitising" the societal record without visible censorship.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Stage a controlled crisis at a contained site with a managed cast of participants.
2. Run live information operations: shape coverage, seed narrative, suppress inconvenient signal.
3. Measure how cleanly the engineered version of events becomes the accepted version.
4. Encode the resulting playbook into the AI network for permanent operation.

## 4. Claimed benefits

- Proves control of the **digital context** — the decisive battleground of the coming century.
- Converts censorship from a blunt, detectable act into an invisible curation of "what is true."
- Hands the Patriots a self-sustaining engine for societal stability.

## 5. Known risks (sponsor's own assessment)

- If the exercise is exposed, the illusion of a free, self-governing public collapses publicly.
- The cast and operators learn too much; each is a potential leak.
- A doctrine this powerful could be captured and turned by whoever holds the apparatus.
- "Sanitising" the record may erode the very legitimacy that makes the population compliant.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council sanction the S³ Plan as the proving ground for total context control, and
under what safeguards?
