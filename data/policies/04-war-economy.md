# Proposal: The **War Economy** — Perpetual Managed Conflict

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** TR (Force & War Economy)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

Peace is the one outcome the Patriots cannot afford. A world at peace stops spending, stops fearing, and
stops needing us. We propose to formalise the **War Economy**: a globally distributed system of private
military companies, proxy conflicts, and managed escalation that keeps the world in a permanent state of
*controlled* war — never large enough to end civilisation, never small enough to end demand.

Conflict becomes the load-bearing engine of the entire system of control.

## 2. Objectives

- Stand up a network of Patriot-aligned PMCs that supply, staff, and profit from regulated conflict.
- Engineer a steady cadence of proxy wars — large enough to drive spending, contained enough to manage.
- Make the global economy structurally dependent on continuous, low-grade warfare.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Capitalise and quietly control the leading PMCs on multiple sides of each conflict.
2. Tune the intensity dial: escalate where demand sags, de-escalate before any war becomes decisive.
3. Recycle the same soldiers, weapons, and grievances through an endless rotation of theatres.
4. Bind defence budgets, markets, and politics to the assumption that war is permanent.

## 4. Claimed benefits

- A **self-funding** engine for Patriot control that pays for every other plan on the docket.
- Total leverage over states that depend on the conflict cycle for their economies.
- A pressure valve: discontent and ambition are channelled into managed war rather than against us.

## 5. Known risks (sponsor's own assessment)

- Perpetual war means perpetual suffering — a moral and narrative liability if the design is exposed.
- The intensity dial can slip; a managed war can become an unmanageable one.
- Soldiers ground down by endless rotation may become the seed of revolt.
- A system that requires war forecloses the very peace the Boss is said to have dreamed of.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council sanction the War Economy as the financial engine of Patriot control, and with
what limits on escalation?
