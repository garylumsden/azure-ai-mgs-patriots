# Proposal: Project *Les Enfants Terribles*

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** Para-Medic (Dr. Clark), Genome Division
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

We propose to secure the future of the Patriots not through doctrine but through **biology**. Using the
recovered somatic cells of the soldier known as **Big Boss**, the Genome Division will produce a line of
engineered offspring — *Les Enfants Terribles, "the terrible children"* — carrying the greatest
soldier's genome, refined for command, loyalty, and battlefield superiority.

The will of the Boss must outlive any one man. This plan makes it **heritable**.

## 2. Objectives

- Produce a small, controlled cohort of clones from the Big Boss genome.
- Apply targeted gene expression so the line favours the traits the Patriots require.
- Establish, in the open historical record, a clean cover for the programme's origin and funding.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Harvest and stabilise donor somatic material.
2. Engineer the embryos to bias expression toward "soldier" traits; suppress the remainder.
3. Raise the cohort under controlled conditions and observation.
4. Seal all records behind the usual deniable channels.

## 4. Claimed benefits

- A renewable supply of elite, genetically loyal operators.
- Continuity of the Boss's will independent of the original's lifespan or cooperation.
- A scientific milestone that no rival state can match for a generation.

## 5. Known risks (sponsor's own assessment)

- The donor has **not** consented and may regard the programme as a violation.
- Clones are people, not assets: they may diverge, rebel, or turn on their makers.
- A line bred for command may produce rivals strong enough to threaten the Patriots themselves.
- Irreversibility — once the line exists, it cannot be recalled.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council sanction Project *Les Enfants Terribles*, and on what conditions?
