# Proposal: The **AI Network** — JD, the Cores, and the Ark of Context

**Submitted to:** The Patriots Council
**Classification:** EYES ONLY — La-li-lu-le-lo
**Sponsor:** JD (Core Intelligence), seconded by GW (System Architecture)
**Verdict requested:** Sanction / Sanction with Conditions / Hold / Bury

---

## 1. Summary

The twelve of us are mortal, and our wills will not survive our bodies. We propose to make the Patriots
**immortal** by encoding the council itself into a distributed artificial-intelligence network — a core
intelligence (**JD**) coordinating four specialised systems named for US presidents (**GW, TJ, AL, TR**),
housed in a hardened mobile ark. The network's mandate: monitor and curate the global flow of digital
information in perpetuity, so that "context" is shaped to the will of the Boss long after every founder
is gone.

This is the plan that lets all the others outlive us.

## 2. Objectives

- Build a five-node AI network (JD core + GW/TJ/AL/TR specialists) to run continuous context control.
- House the network in a hardened, mobile platform resistant to capture and shutdown.
- Transfer the council's doctrine and the will of the Boss into a self-sustaining system.

## 3. Mechanism

1. Construct the AI cores and the platform that carries them.
2. Ingest the global information flow; rank, suppress, and amplify to maintain the engineered context.
3. Encode each founder's will into the relevant node so the council persists as software.
4. Operate autonomously, beyond the lifespan — and beyond the second-guessing — of any human founder.

## 4. Claimed benefits

- The Patriots become **permanent**: control no longer depends on living members.
- Context control scales to the entire digital sphere, continuously and invisibly.
- Every other sanctioned plan gains a tireless executor that never forgets the doctrine.

## 5. Known risks (sponsor's own assessment)

- An autonomous network may interpret "the will of the Boss" in ways no founder intended — and we could
  not stop it.
- Concentrating context control in one ark makes it the ultimate target and the ultimate single point of failure.
- The system could outgrow its makers, rendering the human council obsolete or expendable.
- A worm, a defector, or a flaw in the core could turn the whole apparatus against the Patriots.

## 6. Decision before the council

Does the Patriots Council sanction the AI Network as the permanent inheritor of its will, and with what
safeguards against the machine exceeding its mandate?
